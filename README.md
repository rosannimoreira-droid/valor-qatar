# Valor Qatar — Haute Chocolaterie & Bespoke Floristry

Official digital showcase and organized product media archive for **Valor Qatar** (Sendian Group), Doha, Qatar.

## Highlights

- **Site Architecture & Aesthetic:** Combines Qatar luxury e-commerce taxonomy (Flowers, Chocolates, Combos, Dates & Traditional Sweets, Savory & Warak Enab, Celebration Cakes, Occasions, Majlis Stands & Towers) with a warm organic editorial aesthetic.
- **Bilingual Support:** Instant English (`LTR`) and Arabic (`RTL`) language switching.
- **Interactive Features:**
  - Circular category story discovery bar
  - Multi-facet filtering (category, occasion, color, price sort, live search)
  - Quick-view modal with size selectors (*Original*, *Deluxe*, *Grand Imperial*) and custom Arabic acrylic calligraphy toppers
  - Custom Majlis Stand & Bouquet Builder with real-time QAR estimation
  - Direct WhatsApp Concierge ordering (`+974 5999 2926`)
